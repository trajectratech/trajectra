## Long version (~420 words)

**Trajectra Technologies — custom software teams for companies that need to
ship.**

Trajectra is a software engineering company based in Lagos, Nigeria, working
with clients worldwide. We build new products, modernise systems that have
become difficult to change, and embed senior engineers into existing product
teams.

**What we do**

*Build a product.* A working, production-deployed application — not a
prototype. Web app, API, infrastructure, CI, and a handover that lets your own
team take it over. Typically 8–12 weeks at a fixed scope and a fixed price.

*Modernise what you have.* Framework upgrades, cloud migration, performance work
and test coverage on systems you cannot afford to rewrite. Every engagement
starts with a two-week assessment and a written rewrite-versus-refactor
recommendation — including the times we conclude a rewrite is not necessary.

*Embed a team.* Named senior engineers working inside your process — your
standups, your issue tracker, your repository. Monthly, with a three-month
minimum and thirty days' notice to stop.

We also run technical training, technology advisory and network design work.

**How we work, and what you can hold us to**

We publish our commercial terms rather than making clients ask for them:

- **You own the code.** Full IP assignment on final payment. No licence-back,
  no retained rights.
- **Your repository from day one.** We commit to your GitHub organisation, not
  ours. Nothing is ever held hostage.
- **Fixed price, agreed before anything starts.** Scope changes go through a
  written change order. You will never receive an invoice you were not
  expecting.
- **Thirty-day exit on retainers.** No annual lock-in, no penalty.

We will also tell you when not to build something. If we think a project will
not solve the problem you have, or that another team is a better fit, that is
what the first call will say. We would rather lose an engagement early than
hand over something you cannot use.

**Practical details**

We work West Africa Time (UTC+1), which gives a full working day of overlap
with Europe, the UK and Africa, a wide afternoon window with the Americas, and a
morning window with the Gulf and South Asia. We run standups on your calendar
rather than ours.

Invoicing is by bank transfer in USD, EUR, GBP or NGN — against milestones for
fixed-scope projects, monthly for retainers.

Trajectra Technologies is incorporated in Nigeria, registration number
**RC 8464542**, verifiable on the Corporate Affairs Commission register.

Start with a 30-minute call: **trajectra.com**

## Notes on using this

**Trustpilot's rules matter here.** You cannot incentivise reviews in any way —
no discounts, no gifts, no preferential treatment — and you cannot selectively
invite only happy clients. Both are grounds for a warning label on the profile,
which is far worse than having few reviews. Invite every completed client, in
the same way, every time.

**Trustpilot is probably not your primary review platform.** It is
consumer-weighted, and software buyers evaluating an agency look at **Clutch**
first, then G2. Clutch reviews are conducted as verified interviews, which
carries far more weight with a CTO than a star rating. Do both if you like, but
if effort is limited, Clutch is the one that affects whether you win contracts.

**Do not add an `aggregateRating` to the website** until real reviews exist and
are visible on the page. Fabricated ratings are a Google structured-data policy
violation and one was already removed from this site during the audit.
